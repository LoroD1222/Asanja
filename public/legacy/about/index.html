Asanja Story

Welcome to Asanja Africa, where every safari is a celebration of love, courage, and the untamed beauty of the African wilderness. Derived from the Maasai word for “LOVER,” Asanja pays tribute to the romantic sagas of rebellious warriors who dared to follow their hearts.

In Maasai culture, boys on the cusp of manhood embark on a journey to become warriors, entrusted with the security of their community.

Asanja Africa is a proud family-owned business, born from passion in 2008. Our inaugural camp, Asanja Moru – Luxury Bush Camp, opened its doors in 2010 with a singular mission: to offer an Asanja experience, not just a night in the wilderness.

Senergeti & Ruaha National Parks

Explore our Grounds

In the heart of Tanzania’s Serengeti National Park and Ruaha National Park, our camps and lodges provide access to both the Northern and Southern circuits. Witness the Great Migration and explore diverse ecosystems, all while enjoying the comfort of one of our four luxurious properties.

Our promise

Asanja Commitment

Welcome to Asanja Africa, where our commitment to sustainability and community empowerment is as breathtaking as the landscapes surrounding us. Nestled amidst Tanzania’s pristine wilderness, our camp stands as a beacon of responsible tourism, carefully designed to minimize our footprint on the environment we cherish.

From the outset, we have dedicated ourselves to preserving the natural balance of our surroundings. Our camp harmonizes seamlessly with the wilderness, with no permanent structures disrupting the untouched beauty of the area. Not a single tree has been sacrificed in our endeavor to create a haven where luxury meets conservation.

But our dedication to sustainability extends beyond our physical footprint. We believe in uplifting the communities that call this land home. That’s why the majority of our dedicated staff are sourced from neighboring communities, providing economic opportunities through tourism and ensuring a genuine connection to the land they share with you.

At Asanja Africa, we invite you to indulge in the splendor of the wilderness, knowing that your comfort is matched by our commitment to responsible travel. Whether you’re relaxing in our cozy accommodations or embarking on an unforgettable safari adventure, our team is devoted to surpassing your expectations at every turn.

Join us in discovering “a little bit of paradise in the bush” and experience the magic of Asanja firsthand.

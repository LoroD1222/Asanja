Welcome to Asanja Moru

In the Heart of Serengeti

Asanja Moru

Welcome to Asanja Moru, where luxury seamlessly intertwines with eco-consciousness amidst the stunning expanse of the Serengeti. Established in 2010 as Asanja’s pioneering camp, our dedication to preserving the natural world lies at the very heart of our ethos. Crafted exclusively with reusable materials and devoid of concrete, Asanja Moru stands as a beacon of sustainable, low-impact luxury in the wilderness.

About Asanja Moru

Discover a landscape of luxury

Asanja Moru is situated within the Moru area of Serengeti National Park, a mere 40-minute drive from the Seronera Airstrip. Renowned for hosting The Great Migration, where vast herds of wildebeests and zebras traverse annually, this location offers an unparalleled wildlife experience.

INTIMATE LUXURY

Asanja Moru offers an exclusive and intimate experience with just eight tents, ensuring personalized service and tranquility.

STORYTELLING THROUGH DESIGN

Each tent is meticulously designed to reflect a specific Maasai ceremony, offering a unique blend of luxury and cultural immersion.

HISTORIC SIGNIFICANCE

Asanja Moru is positioned near the Gong Rock, a site adorned with ancient Maasai paintings. Immerse yourself in the rich tapestry of the region’s cultural heritage.

UNFORGETTABLE ENCOUNTERS

Witness the drama unfold as predators stalk prey from your private deck, or enjoy close-up encounters with a pride of lions lazing nearby.

BEYOND THE DRIVE

Asanja Moru goes beyond traditional game drives. Explore the surrounding landscape on guided nature walks, uncovering hidden wonders and deepening your connection with the wilderness.

Location

SERENGETI NATIONAL PARK

MORU AREA: Asanja Moru is situated within the Moru area of Serengeti National Park, a mere 40-minute drive from the Seronera Airstrip. Renowned for hosting The Great Migration, where vast herds of wildebeests and zebras traverse annually, this location offers an unparalleled wildlife experience. The area is teeming with resident animals, including the iconic Big Five and the intriguing Ugly Five!

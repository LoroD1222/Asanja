Welcome to Asanja Nyasirori

Western corridor of the Serengeti

Asanja Nyasirori

Unveiling a new chapter in luxury safaris, Asanja Nyasirori beckons in the western corridor of Serengeti National Park. Inspired by the Maasai’s traditional enkaji huts, our seven individual luxury bomas and one two-bedroom family suite offer unparalleled comfort. Open year-round, this wildlife haven boasts resident animals and breathtaking scenery. Immerse yourself in the Maasai spirit with our intriguing boma, featuring three interconnected huts with private plunge pools, a lounge area, and a dining space for ultimate privacy. Witness the awe-inspiring Great Migration as wildebeest thunder across the nearby Grumeti River, teeming with hippos, crocodiles, and rare Colobus monkeys. Asanja Nyasirori, nestled amidst the open woodlands of Nyasirori Plains, offers a luxurious gateway to unforgettable wildlife encounters.

Location

SERENGETI NATIONAL PARK

SERENGETI AREA: Nestled in the heart of the Western Serengeti, Asanja Grumeti offers a prime location just a 10-minute drive from the iconic Grumeti River, a vital artery during the Great Migration. Witness the awe-inspiring spectacle of wildebeest and zebra thundering across the waters, a scene that unfolds right on your doorstep. While the resident crocodile population waits patiently, the relentless tide of life ensures the drama continues. This wildlife haven also boasts a wealth of resident species, from hippos and monkeys frolicking in the water to majestic elephants enjoying a refreshing drink.

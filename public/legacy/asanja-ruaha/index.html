Welcome to Asanja Ndembo, Ruaha

Just off the Great Ruaha River

Asanja Ndembo

Escape to Asanja Ndembo, a luxurious retreat located off the banks of the majestic Ruaha River in Tanzania. Enjoy unparalleled luxury and breathtaking views in the wilderness heart of Tanzania, with close proximity to Ruaha National Park’ s vibrant ecosystem. Discover the magic of the Ruaha River and the wild wonders of Ruaha National Park at Asanja Ndembo Ruaha, where you can find the perfect blend of luxury, tranquility, and adventure in one of Africa’s most captivating destinations.

About Asanja Ndembo

Discover Ruaha's Hidden Gems

Unveiling the untamed beauty of Ruaha National Park, Tanzania’s largest, Asanja Ndembo curates an unparalleled private safari experience. Immerse yourself in diverse activities like walking safaris and sundowners, returning to the luxurious comfort of your lodge, Asanja Ndembo, at the end of each unforgettable day.

Unveiling Ruaha's Secrets

Asanja Ndembo offers exclusive access to Ruaha National Park, Tanzania’s largest, where you can delve deeper than ever before with expertly guided adventures.

Beyond the Drive

Embark on diverse safaris, venturing beyond traditional game drives. Explore the wilderness on foot with walking safaris, or soak in the fiery hues of the sunset with unforgettable sundowner experiences.

Luxurious Comfort Awaits

With eight exquisitely appointed luxury suites, each evening invites you to return to the opulent comfort of your room, where elegance meets tranquility. Indulge in lavish accommodations thoughtfully designed to offer the ultimate in relaxation and rejuvenation after a day of exhilarating adventures.

Sustainable Practices

Immerse yourself in the beauty of Ruaha while embracing responsible tourism. Asanja Ndembo prioritizes sustainable practices, ensuring the long-term health of this breathtaking wilderness.

Your Curated Adventure

Our dedicated safari specialists collaborate with you to craft a personalized itinerary that reflects your desires. Whether you seek heart-pounding encounters or moments of tranquil observation, Asanja Ndembo curates your dream safari.

CAMP ACTIVITIES

Professionally guided Game Drives, Walking Safaris, Big 5 Safaris, Bird Watching, Bush Meals, Sundowners, Professional Photographer, Intimate Weddings & Honeymoon

Location

RUAHA NATIONAL PARK

Tanzania’s hidden gem, Ruaha National Park, beckons with a pristine wilderness unlike any other in East Africa. As the largest park in the region, Ruaha boasts over 10,000 elephants and the world’s second-highest concentration of lions. Escape the crowds and truly immerse yourself in a real African paradise. Here, elusive endangered wild dogs roam free, making Ruaha a haven for predators and an unforgettable safari destination for those seeking a more rewarding experience.

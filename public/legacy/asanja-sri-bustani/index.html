Western corridor of the Serengeti

Asanja Siri Bustani

Unveiling the magic of the Serengeti, Asanja Siri Bustani beckons you to an unforgettable love story with the wild. Established in 2015, this intimate retreat in Serengeti National Park offers an exclusive adventure. Asanja Siri Bustani, your luxurious basecamp, features just three lavish tented accommodations, each meticulously crafted with local materials for unparalleled comfort. Unwind on your private veranda overlooking the marsh, where the wilderness unfolds before you, seamlessly blending indulgence with breathtaking savannah surroundings. At Asanja Siri Bustani, sustainability meets opulence, creating an unforgettable escape into Tanzania’s untamed beauty.

Location

SERENGETI NATIONAL PARK

SERENGETI AREA: Nestled in the heart of the Western Serengeti, Asanja Siri Bustani offers a prime location just a 10-minute drive from the iconic Grumeti River, a vital artery during the Great Migration. Witness the awe-inspiring spectacle of wildebeest and zebra thundering across the waters, a scene that unfolds right on your doorstep. While the resident crocodile population waits patiently, the relentless tide of life ensures the drama continues. This wildlife haven also boasts a wealth of resident species, from hippos and monkeys frolicking in the water to majestic elephants enjoying a refreshing drink.

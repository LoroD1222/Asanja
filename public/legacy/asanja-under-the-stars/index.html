Asanja Under the Stars

Embrace the untamed spirit of the Serengeti with Asanja’s Under the Stars fly camping experience. Sleep beneath a canopy of a million stars, lulled by the nocturnal symphony of the African bush. This exclusive adventure takes you far from the established camps, placing you in the heart of the wilderness. Expertly guided bush walks and thrilling game drives fill your days, while evenings are spent around a crackling campfire, sharing stories and witnessing the breathtaking African sunset. Asanja’s meticulous attention to detail ensures your comfort, with luxurious amenities like comfortable bedding and en-suite facilities transforming your fly camp into an unforgettable oasis under the vast African sky.

About Asanja Under the Stars

An exclusive adventure

Asanja Under the Stars is a unique and serene experience offered by Asanja Africa, where guests can spend a night under the African skies in a luxurious outdoor bed. This experience takes place in a secluded spot in the Serengeti National Park, far away from the main camp, providing a truly private and exclusive experience.

Unforgettable Stargazing

Asanja’s Under the Stars fly camping experience offers an unparalleled opportunity to sleep beneath a canopy of millions of stars. Witness the breathtaking night sky in all its glory, far from light pollution, and reconnect with the untamed beauty of the wilderness.

Unwind in the Heart of the Serengeti

Venture beyond established camps and immerse yourself in the raw beauty of the Serengeti. This exclusive adventure places you front and center to the sights and sounds of the African bush.

Expertly Guided Exploration

Embark on unforgettable adventures with our experienced guides. Explore the diverse landscapes on thrilling game drives and fascinating bush walks, uncovering the secrets of the Serengeti.

Campfire Stories Under the Stars

Gather around a crackling campfire as the sun dips below the horizon. Share stories, unwind under the vast expanse of the African night sky, and witness the awe-inspiring African sunset. Asanja creates unforgettable memories beneath the Milky Way.

Comfort in the Wilderness

Asanja prioritizes your comfort even under the stars. Enjoy en-suite facilities and luxurious amenities within your mobile camp, transforming your fly camping experience into a unique and unforgettable adventure.

Camp Activities

Professionally guided Game Drives, Walking Safaris, Balloon Safaris, Big 5 Safaris, Cultural Visits to nearby communities, Day trips to Ngorongoro Crater, Bird Watching, Maasai Village, Bush Meals, Sundowners, Professional Photographer, Intimate Weddings & Honeymoon
Ameneties

Included in your stay

Asanja Under the Stars is a secluded private fly camp, offering an exclusive and romantic escape in a breathtaking remote location. Your stay includes a luxurious en-suite tent, adorned with elegant touches, and exquisite candlelit meals beneath the starlit sky. To complete the experience, you’ll have the dedicated service of your private butler and ranger, ensuring an unforgettable journey into the heart of the wilderness.

IN THE HEART OF THE WILD

Escape the confines of permanent camps and delve deeper into the heart of the Serengeti with Asanja’s Under the Stars fly camping experience. This exclusive adventure strategically relocates your basecamp throughout your stay, placing you in the most prime wildlife viewing locations each night. Imagine waking up to the sounds of the African bush just outside your tent, or drifting off to sleep under a canopy of a million stars, far from any light pollution. Asanja Under the Stars allows you to experience the raw, untamed beauty of the Serengeti in a way that is both thrilling and undeniably intimate.

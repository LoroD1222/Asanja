Our Luxury Camps

Immerse yourself in unparalleled luxury amidst Tanzania’s wilderness with Asanja’s award-winning tented camps. Breathtaking scenery and unforgettable stories waiting to be discovered, choose your perfect escape.

The Serengeti Spectacle

Asanja Moru

Asanja Moru, nestled amidst the dramatic Rocky Islands known as Moru Kopjes, offers a front-row seat to the awe-inspiring Serengeti spectacle.

Discover

An Intimate Escape

Asanja Siri Bustani

Asanja Siri Bustani, a sanctuary of just three tents, offers an intimate escape where you can witness the awe-inspiring spectacle of the Great Migration in luxurious comfort.

Discover

Luxury in the Wild West

Asanja Nyasirori

Nestled amidst the wildlife-rich western corridor, Asanja Nyasirori invites you to experience unparalleled luxury in exquisitely designed Maasai-inspired bomas.

Discover

Majestic Riverside Retreat

Asanja Ndembo, Ruaha

Asanja Ndembo, perched majestically along the Great Ruaha River, offers front-row seats to Tanzania’s captivating wildlife spectacle.

Asanja Under the stars

Asanja Under the Stars offers the ultimate indulgence: an exclusive fly-camping experience beneath a canopy of stars, where wilderness becomes your private sanctuary.

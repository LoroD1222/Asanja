General Inquiries

What locations does Asanja offer safaris in?

Asanja offers luxurious safaris in two prime locations within Tanzania:

  1. Central Serengeti  and Grumeti, The Western corridor of the Serengeti National Park, known for the Great Migration.
  2. Ruaha National Park, one of the largest national parks in East Africa.

Serengeti National Park:
Asanja Moru, Central southern, (45 mins from Seronera airstrip)
Asanja Siri Bustani – Grumeti, Western Serengeti (35mins from Grumeti Airstrip)
Asanja Nyasirori – Grumeti, Western Serengeti (35 mins from Grumeti Airstrip)

Ruaha National Park:
Asanja Ndembo (1 hour away from Msembe Airstrip)

When is the best time to visit for a safari?

Both locations offer year-round safaris with unique experiences depending on the season.

The Serengeti is particularly known for the Great Migration, typically occurring from July to October. The calving season takes place from January to March, where you can witness around 500,000 calves being born and taking their first steps is another great phenomenon.

What are the different types of safari experiences offered by Asanja?

Asanja curates a variety of experiences to suit your desires:

  • Classic game drives in open vehicles for prime wildlife viewing.
  • Walking safaris for a more intimate exploration on foot.
  • Family-oriented adventures with our engaging Asanja Little Explorers program.
  • Under the Stars Experience – one of a kind.

Accommodation & Amenities

What kind of accommodation does Asanja offer?

Asanja provides luxurious tented camps and lodges with spacious en-suite facilities and plush furnishings.

At Asanja Nyasirori, Serengeti and Asanja Ndembo, Ruaha, guests stay in spacious en-suite luxury accommodation, each with its own private plunge pool.

What amenities are included in my stay?

Asanja offers a variety of amenities to ensure your comfort and enjoyment:

  • Twice-daily housekeeping and laundry service
  • Private verandas with comfortable seating (most locations)
  • En-suite bathrooms with luxurious amenities
  • In-room amenities like toiletries, slippers, and robes
  • High-speed Wi-Fi access in designated camp areas (varies by location)
  • Access to a swimming pool, fitness center, or stargazing platform (varies by location)
  • Secure guest safes and power backup systems

Wildlife & Sustainability

What wildlife can I expect to see on safari?

The Serengeti is particularly known for the Great Migration, typically occurring from July to October. The calving season takes place from January to March, where you can witness around 500,000 calves being born and taking their first steps is another great phenomenon.

Ruaha National Park is wild, vast, remote and untouched by crowds. Here, 10% of the world’s lion population roams in unusually large prides. Wild dogs add to the thrill, alongside the large herds of elephants and buffaloes. Other animals seen are jackals, clans of hyenas, zebra, giraffe, and over 574 bird species, are recorded to date.

What is Asanja's commitment to sustainability?

Asanja is committed to responsible tourism practices that protect the environment and support local communities. We utilize eco-friendly practices in our camps, minimize our footprint, and work with local conservation initiatives.

Can I participate in any wildlife conservation efforts during my stay?

Absolutely! Asanja offers optional opportunities to learn more about conservation efforts and contribute to the protection of Tanzania’s wildlife and ecosystems. Speak with our knowledgeable staff about these options during your stay.
